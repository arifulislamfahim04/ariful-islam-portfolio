# Typefaces

Fraunces and DM Sans, downloaded from the official google/fonts repository, ofl/fraunces and ofl/dmsans. SIL Open Font License 1.1; licence files included. Locally subset to Latin and general punctuation in WOFF format. Self-hosted; no third-party font requests.

Fraunces is instantiated at weight 500, optical size 72, softness 20, wonk 1 before subsetting (26 KB). DM Sans keeps its variable weights (83 KB).
